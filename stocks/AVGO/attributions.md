**Agent read.** AVGO is the structurally healthiest name in the AI capex cluster on a 30-day view: +7.4% over 30 days while every other supplier is flat to deeply negative (NVDA +2.1%, AMD -4.0%, TSM -10.3%, MRVL -28.4%, INTC -28.3%). Forward PE 21.6 sits in the middle of the cluster — more expensive than NVDA at 16.5 but far cheaper than AMD at 38 or INTC at 49, and the premium over NVDA is itself informative: the market is paying MORE for the ASIC enabler than for the GPU leader. volr 0.77x means no volume confirmation, consistent with the whole tape. Next verifiable data point is the 9/2-9/3 Q3 FY26 print, where custom-silicon order commentary is the item that matters.
