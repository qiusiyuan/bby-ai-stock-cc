> **One-line thesis.** NAND-flash pure-play riding the AI-storage super-cycle; bull = AI demand + HBF architecture re-rate it from boom-bust commodity into a structural cash machine. **Wrong if** NAND behaves like every prior NAND cycle — 84.6% gross margins are a peak, and a 6x forward P/E on peak earnings is what a top looks like, not a bargain.

## What it does

Makes NAND-flash storage — SSDs, embedded flash, memory cards — for datacenters, OEMs and consumers. Spun out of Western Digital as a NAND pure-play; the datacenter/AI-storage segment is now the growth engine, and it co-owns the new HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) AI-memory standard with SK Hynix.
